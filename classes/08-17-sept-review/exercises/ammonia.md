# Ammonia volatilization

A thin layer of animal slurry is spread on an agricultural field for use as fertilizer.
Given a free ammonia ((NH3 (aq)) concentration of 10 mg/L and a mass transfer coefficient of 0.01 m/s, what is the initial rate of ammonia volatilization (flux)?
What is the initial mass flow rate if the slurry has a surface area of 1 ha (10,000 square meters)?
What would happen to the liquid-side profile and flux over time if

* the slurry layer is very still, with no mixing, or
* the slurry layer is well-mixed below a thin film at the interface?

Given a total ammonia concentration of 3 g/L in the slurry, would you expect that initial flux to persist for days?

If all liquid-side resistance to transport is in a 100 micron layer at the slurry surface, is it reasonable to assume steady-state within that film?
